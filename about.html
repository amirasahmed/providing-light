About Providing Light

A youth program of The Muslim Center of Greater Princeton.

What is Providing Light?

Providing Light is a youth program of The Muslim Center of Greater Princeton for Muslim kids ages 11 to 17. It was created to help young Muslims in the community feel connected, supported, and proud of who they are, especially kids who don't always have a place where they feel they belong.

The program brings kids together through events and online discussion boards where they can make friends, share ideas, and ask questions using anonymous names. Trusted adults from the community are there to guide conversations and help kids grow in their faith.

Every account is created with a parent's permission, and all conversations are watched over by approved moderators.

For parents

We know handing your child an online space is a big decision. Here's how we keep it safe:

  • You create your child's account. One account per child, so nobody can sneak in with a second profile.
  • Trusted program leaders read every board and step in when something isn't right.
  • No private messages. Every conversation happens out in the open.
  • Anonymous, but accountable. Kids use made-up names, but every account has a permanent ID behind the scenes, so warnings follow the account, not the name.
  • Private program boards are only for kids in that program day, who get the code in person.
