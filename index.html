What is Providing Light?

Providing Light is a youth program for young Muslims ages 11–17. We meet in person for study circles, workshops, volunteering, and plenty of fun.

This website keeps our community going between meetings. It's a place to ask the questions you might not ask out loud, share what's on your mind, and cheer each other on, all without using your real name.

Like a lantern, we want to be a steady, warm light: somewhere to find answers, friends, and a sense of belonging.

How we keep everyone safe

Mistakes happen. Our system gently guides kids first, and gets a trusted adult involved when it really matters.

  1. 1

    🧹 Filter

    Bad words are hidden with *** before anyone sees them.

  2. 2

    🙂 Reminder

    A friendly, private reminder about our guidelines. No punishment.

  3. 3

    ⏸️ Short break

    Three slips in one day pauses posting for 10 minutes, and a leader gets a quiet note.

  4. 4

    🛡️ Leader steps in

    Bullying, threats, hate, personal info, or signs a kid is in danger go straight to a trusted adult.

  • 🙈
    Stay anonymous. Never share your real name, school, phone number, address, or social media. Posts with personal info are blocked automatically.
  • 🏷️
    Pick your own name. Shuffle for a new anonymous name like "Quiet River" once every 30 days. Your account stays the same behind the scenes, so warnings don't reset with a new name.
  • 👀
    Everything is out in the open. There are no private messages. All conversations are watched over by trusted leaders.
  • ⏳
    New here? Your first 2 posts are checked by a moderator before they go live. After that, post freely.
  • 🚩
    Report anything wrong. Tap the 🚩 on any post that makes you uncomfortable.
  • 🆘
    In danger or need help now? Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency.

For parents

We know handing your child an online space is a big decision. Here's how we keep it safe:

  • You create your child's account. One account per child, so nobody can sneak in with a second profile.
  • Trusted program leaders read every board and step in when something isn't right.
  • No private messages. Every conversation happens out in the open.
  • Anonymous, but accountable. Kids use made-up names, but every account has a permanent ID behind the scenes, so warnings follow the account, not the name.
  • Private program boards are only for kids in that program day, who get the code in person.
