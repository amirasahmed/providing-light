Staying safe

  • 🙈
    Stay anonymous. Never share your real name, school, phone number, address, or social media. The chat blocks phone numbers and emails automatically.
  • 💛
    Be kind. Treat everyone the way you want to be treated. Mean or unsafe messages are removed.
  • 👀
    A leader is always watching. A trusted adult from the program reads the chats to keep everyone safe.
  • 🚩
    Report anything wrong. Tap the 🚩 on any message that makes you uncomfortable.
  • 🆘
    In danger or need help now? Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or call 911 in an emergency.
